Tessa & Deepak — Engagement Invitation

This is a separate engagement invitation designed to share the wedding website's envelope interaction and typography, while following the original engagement card's peach, blush, ivory, terracotta and navy palette. There are no green accents.

Preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js and the assets folder together.
3. Open index.html in Chrome.

Google Fonts are loaded online, so an internet connection is needed for the exact typography. The countdown is set to 26 December 2026 at 11:45 AM IST.
